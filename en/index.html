The remainder taken for the number

A counter that rolls back to zero keeps the position and throws away the turns. An airliner, satellites and a radiotherapy machine have taken that remainder for the number. The ARC corpus starts from there: it names the gesture, gives the formulas for what it costs, and shows the writing that throws nothing away.

What the tool threw away

1987 · Yakima

The one-byte counter

In the Therac-25 radiotherapy machine, a one-byte variable rolls back to zero on its 256th pass. At zero, a check is skipped. A patient receives an overdose and dies of it.

255 + 1 → 0: the turn is thrown away

Leveson and Turner, Computer, 1993.

2015 · Boeing 787

The airliner that shuts down after 248 days

An internal counter overflows after 248 days under power, and the aircraft can lose all its electrical power, whatever the phase of flight.

2³¹ hundredths of a second = 248.55 days

FAA, directive AD 2015-09-07.

1999 and 2019 · GPS

Twenty years back

The week number is held on ten binary digits and rolls back to zero every 1,024 weeks. A receiver that takes this remainder for the date believes it is 19.6 years earlier.

1,024 weeks = 19.6 years

GPS interface specification, IS-GPS-200, § 20.3.3.3.1.1.

1991 · Dhahran

0.34 second

One tenth of a second, whose binary writing never ends, is cut at 24 digits. After a hundred hours, the Patriot missile's clock has drifted by 0.34 s. The interception misses: 28 dead. This is the other gesture, the truncated decimal.

0.000000095 × 10 × 3600 × 100 = 0.34 s

GAO, IMTEC-92-26, 1992.

1961 · Lorenz

0.506 for 0.506127

Lorenz restarts his calculation by retyping three decimals instead of six. The simulated weather diverges. It was made into a property of nature; it was a truncated seed.

starting gap: 0.000127

American Physical Society, APS News, January 2003.

2038 · Dated prediction

19 January, 03:14:07

Seconds counted on 32 binary digits reach their limit. Any uncorrected system that rolls back will show 13 December 1901 at that second.

2³¹ − 1 seconds after 1 January 1970

Fall criterion: a system of this kind that does not jump.

What it costs

The three formulas of chaos

ε(n) = ε₀ · fⁿ

I. The truncated seed

Stevin, 1585. One third is written 0.3333: 1/30,000 is missing. Multiplied by 3 at each step, with nothing folded, the gap reaches 10⁴³ in a hundred steps.

II. The fold

The remainder, Gauss, 1801. The measurement is exact, but at each step the position is kept and the turn thrown away. Two starts, 1/5 and 1/5 + 1/256: at step 8, same position, and turns that differ by one, 51 and 52.

III. The machine

It truncates and folds at each step. This is the chaos we measure, and it is the one we manufactured.

Independent confirmation: in exact arithmetic, with no numerical drift at all, the orbit remains chaotic (Saito and Yamaguchi, Chaos, 2018). The chaos of the fold does not come from rounding: it comes from the thrown-away turn.

The Great Pyramid of Khufu

The slope is given by the builders themselves: a seked of 5½ palms for a cubit of 7 palms. The height is therefore 14/11 of the half-base, and the perimeter is 44/7 of the height, exactly.

The figure of the corpus, two circles and a square, folded into a pyramid, gives the same slope to within a few ten-thousandths.

14/11 = 1.2727 · folded figure: 1.2720 · measured: 146.6 / 115.2 = 1.2726

What this does not establish. Petrie's measured dimensions do not decide between 44/7 and 2π: the gap is below his margin of uncertainty. The corpus does not say the Egyptians “knew π”. It says that their slope is a closed ratio, 14/11, and that the figure gives it back.

The figure, laid flat

√2 and the Tsirelson bound

Two circles with the same centre, a square between them. It can be drawn by hand in ten seconds.

The diagonal of the square gives √2. The ratio of the two circles, squared, gives ½. And the bound that quantum mechanics sets on the correlations between two entangled particles can be read on those three strokes:

2√2 = 4 × ½ × √2 · corners × fold × diagonal

Without Hilbert space. Bell's theorem is not disputed. The classical bound measures the sides of the square; the quantum bound measures its diagonal.

The method

Fractal, social and relational empathy

A reading method: enter an era, a situation or a relationship as if living in it, to see power and the code that governs it, the social relations and the psychological state of people, then reread what was seen with today's tools and knowledge. It does not judge first, it recognises first; it looks for the pattern that repeats and carries it from one scale to another.

  1. Enter. Step into the era as if living there, with its means, its fears and its knowledge.
  2. See power and its governing code. Who decides, in the name of what, and what code governs that power.
  3. Read the social relations. Who depends on whom, who risks what by speaking.
  4. Read the psychological state. Fear, ambition, shame, faith; the emotion that became a rule.
  5. Reread with today. Hold the reading of the era and the reading of the present together; what is freed between them is the new.

Order matters: rereading before entering is judging; entering without rereading is storytelling.

What the figure unfolds

A grammar before the theories

There are exactly nine one-digit quantities. This fact precedes every base and every formalism. The ARC corpus studies the structure these nine positions impose, then checks, domain by domain, where that structure governs.

The involution

φ(x) = 9 − x

Four conjugate pairs, (1,8), (2,7), (3,6), (4,5), visible down to the multiplication tables: paired rows sum to 9 column by column.

The constant

k = 4.5 / 9 = 1/2

Derived by seven independent routes: the ratio of the two circles, the barycentre, the half-span, the spherical projection, spin 1/2. The convergence of derivations with no shared axiom is the corpus's criterion of robustness.

Fourier is an ARC

In 1807, while Gauss engraved the remainder, Fourier filed the corpus's form: everything is a sum of closed circles, a fundamental and its integer harmonics. The multiplication table is a Fourier series on the ring, the fundamental figure a drawn series, the atom a series of harmonics (verified at build). Quantization, uncertainty, probability, entropy, the bit: five patches over a form that was refused, then admitted as technique, then stripped of its name. v0.2 adds section 1 bis: the formalisation (Dirichlet, Riemann, Cantor, Lebesgue) as a translation of the circle into the line's language, and the flat-earth legend (1828 to 1896) as the first modulo applied to history.
