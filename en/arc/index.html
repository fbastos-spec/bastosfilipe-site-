ARC Corpus

Architecture of Relational Coherence

« Rien n'est demandé au lecteur sauf de vérifier. Les preuves, pas les paroles. »

The ARC corpus (Architecture of Relational Coherence) rereads mathematics, physics, the living world and the city from a single figure, two circles and a square, and from the constant ½ it imposes; the operation 1 ⊕ 1 = 3 and the cadastre of nine positions follow from it, down to the delfine, the composite of the 6k ± 1 grid read as a descendant and not as a reject. It is published in twelve volumes. Every statement carries its status: Theorem (verified finite computation or proof), Reading (interpretation declared as such), Prediction (falsifiable, with its criterion), Fact, or Open site (open question). The calculations are shown, and the reader is invited to redo them.
