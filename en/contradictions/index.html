Have you found a calculation error, an inconsistency between two statements, a source badly cited, a prediction that falls? Send it. It is the most useful service you can do the corpus.

Every contradiction received is published here, with its date, the statement concerned and the author's answer: corrected, maintained with its reason, withdrawn, or still under review. Nothing is erased: a correction keeps the trace of what it corrects.
