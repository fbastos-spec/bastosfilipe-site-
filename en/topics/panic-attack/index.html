The big questions

Panic attack

Why a panic attack comes for no apparent reason

The short answer

On this reading, a panic attack that comes for no reason is not a false alarm: it is an accurate alarm whose address has been erased. It signals an old and real mark, but shame has erased what it points to; that is why it comes without an object, often at night, and why reasoning does not calm it.

This page offers a philosophical and psychological reading; it is not medical advice and replaces no care. A panic attack can have physical causes (thyroid, heart, blood sugar, medication, withdrawal) that a doctor must rule out first. Validated treatments (cognitive behavioural therapy, exposure, medication) have measured results. In case of chest pain or distress, call emergency services (911 in North America, 112 in Europe).

What clinical practice establishes

FactClinicians distinguish expected attacks, triggered by an identifiable cue, from unexpected attacks with no identifiable trigger; the diagnosis of panic disorder rests on the recurrence of the latter. The person often knows, during the attack, that no danger is present, and that knowledge does not stop it.

The ARC reading

Reading"Fear is what rings; shame is what erased what the ringing pointed to." The attack is fear in its execution and shame in its cause: a normal fear whose address has been erased.

ReadingThe attack unfolds in three stages: an old inscription, often bound up with attachment (an abandonment, a humiliation), accompanied by shame; a daytime arming that nobody notices, a tone, a delay, a look; a discharge at night, when occupation stops and only shame keeps masking.

ReadingThe alarm is not false: it is an error of location. Reasoning fails by construction, because it addresses the present, while the alarm comes from a mark located elsewhere. Work therefore starts with stabilisation, then with restoring addresses.

ReadingPanic must be distinguished from grief. Every great grief produces its own panic, and that panic is healthy because it is addressed: one knows what collapsed, and every collision with the absence corrects the map.

What would bring it down

PredictionThere must be people with low or no anxiety who have recurrent unexpected attacks: if every unexpected attack came with pre-existing anxiety, the proposed separation would fall. The corpus also expects CO₂ sensitivity to decrease once the address is restored.
