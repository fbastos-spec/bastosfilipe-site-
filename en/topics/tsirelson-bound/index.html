The big questions

Tsirelson bound

Why the Tsirelson bound is 2√2

The short answer

The Tsirelson bound, 2√2, is read on a square. The pair read by its sides gives 2; read by its diagonals, 2√2; read by the bare sign, 4. The three follow one another by √2, the ratio of the two circles of the figure. Without Hilbert space, without Bell.

What physics establishes

FactTwo entangled particles, measured far apart, give results more strongly correlated than the ceiling 2 (Bell, 1964). Experiment confirms it, from Aspect (1982) to the loophole-free tests of 2015.

FactThis excess has a limit of its own. Boris Tsirelson established in 1980 that the combination of the four correlations never exceeds 2√2 ≈ 2.828. Received physics obtains this number through the algebra of operators in a Hilbert space. Popescu and Rohrlich showed in 1994 that, without signalling, the algebraic maximum would be 4.

The reading of the ARC corpus

TheoremThe table of outcomes is given for the whole pair: P(A, B | a, b) = ¼ (1 − AB cos θ). It is the same whichever side is written first. The protocol is written with a first and a second line; the pair has none. The correlation that follows is that of the singlet, E = −cos θ, with no wavefunction and no operator.

Reading“Bob does not depend on Alice any more than Alice depends on Bob.” “Nobody gives the direction. The direction is already there, and the entangled other too.” Both at the same time: the pair carries both settings together, through their angle.

TheoremAt the settings 0°, 90°, 45° and 135°, each of the four terms is cos 45° = √2/2, and their sum is 2√2. A scan of all settings, degree by degree, gives nothing larger.

TheoremThree ceilings, one square: the sides give 2, the diagonals 2√2, the bare sign 4. The middle ceiling is √(2 × 4), and 2√2 / 4 = √2/2, the radius of the inscribed circle over that of the circumscribed circle; its square is ½.

Theorem2√2 = 4 × ½ × √2: the four settings of the square, the constant ½, the diagonal. With the side 1 in place of the diagonal, 4 × ½ × 1 = 2. The arithmetic is exact; the identification is a reading of the known bound, not an independent derivation.

TheoremThe same value comes out of the figure a second time, in volume. The star of the figure, folded into a pyramid, has a height equal to 2√2 times its half-base, and a perimeter equal to 2√2 times its height. No length is chosen.

Reading“Classical: measure the sides. Quantum: measure the diagonals.” “They pierced the form and wonder where the balance is, and they forgot to see that Tsirelson is a square.”

What this reading does not say

It changes no measured prediction. It goes through neither Hilbert space nor Bell: it starts from the figure. Nothing travels from one side to the other: each side, taken alone, sees ½, whatever the setting of the other. An outcome is not stored in an object taken alone: it belongs to the relation, the pair and its reading together. The authoritative version is Emergence of Quantum Correlations v4.0 (November 2026); Tsirelson Without Hilbert v4.1 (July 2026) remains the original paper, and its version 4.0, which read the bare sign, is withdrawn with its error published.
